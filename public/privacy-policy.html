Plain-Language Privacy Promise

RoamCeylon & RoamCeylon Admin Privacy Policy

Published by IdeaTech (Pvt) Ltd (Maharagama, Sri Lanka). Written in clear, everyday English so every traveler and local business partner knows what information is collected, how it helps your trip, and how you stay in control.

Effective Date: May 1, 2026 Last Updated: November 14, 2026 Applies To: RoamCeylon App, RoamCeylon Admin & Website

We Never Sell Your Data

Your contact details, saved trips, and location are never sold to advertisers or third-party brokers.

Only Shared for Your Booking

Details are only shared with the verified hotel, guide, activity host, or artisan shop you choose to book with.

Delete Anytime (Within 30 Days)

Delete your account right inside the app or by emailing privacy@roamceylon.com — removed within 30 days.

1. Who This Policy Applies To

This Privacy Policy describes how IdeaTech (Pvt) Ltd (“IdeaTech”, “we”, “our”) handles personal information across:

  • RoamCeylon — the mobile app for travelers exploring Sri Lanka.
  • RoamCeylon Admin — the companion app and portal for verified hotels, tour guides, activity hosts, and craft shops.
  • The RoamCeylon Website — our informational site and online support.

2. Information We Collect

For Travelers (RoamCeylon App)

  • Name, email address, phone number, and optional profile photo.
  • Travel style preferences, saved places, and booking history.
  • Location (only when enabled) to find nearby sights, shops, or emergency help.
  • Reviews or support messages you send inside the app.

For Local Partners (RoamCeylon Admin)

  • Owner name, business phone, email, and bank payout details.
  • Three-step identity check documents: National ID or Passport, business registration certificate, and a live verification selfie.
  • Property photos, room rates, product listings, and tour schedules.

We also keep basic app stability records (crash reports and phone model) so we can fix issues quickly and keep the app reliable.

3. Device Permissions You Control

We only ask for phone permissions when you use a feature that needs them, and you can turn them off anytime in your device settings:

Permission Used In Why We Ask For It
Location RoamCeylon Traveler App Shows nearby attractions and shops on the map, and displays your current address on the Emergency screen.
Camera RoamCeylon Admin Partner App Takes a quick verification selfie and clear photos of partner business licenses.
Photo Library Traveler & Partner Apps Lets partners upload hotel, craft, and tour photos, and lets travelers add a profile photo.
Notifications Traveler & Partner Apps Sends timely booking confirmations, tour reminders, and order updates.

4. How We Use Your Information

  • Fulfill bookings & orders: Confirm stays, guided tours, activities, and Sri Lankan craft purchases.
  • Personalize trip ideas: Suggest the sunniest coasts for your travel month and realistic routes matching your pace.
  • Verify partner businesses: Check business documents so travelers see a trusted Verified Badge.
  • Support & safety: Assist with inquiries, prevent fraud, and provide one-tap access to official Sri Lankan emergency numbers (119, 1990, 110, 1331).

5. How Our Smart Trip Planner Handles Your Data

Your chosen travel style, trip pace, travel month, and saved destinations are used only to generate tailored travel suggestions.

  • Private documents (partner ID cards, business licenses, selfies), passwords, and payment details are never shared with our trip recommendation tool.
  • Trip plans are friendly suggestions that you can edit, reorder, or customize anytime.

6. Who We Share Information With

We never sell your personal data. Essential details are only shared with:

  • Your Chosen Verified Host, Guide, or Shop: Only what is needed to welcome you or deliver your order.
  • Trusted Service & Payment Partners: Companies helping deliver notifications, secure storage, and card payments under strict privacy rules.
  • Legal Authorities: Only when strictly required by Sri Lankan law.

7. How We Protect Your Information

All personal records and verification files are protected with strong industry-standard encryption while moving across the internet and while safely stored. Only authorized IdeaTech specialists reviewing partner applications or helping resolve a customer request can view protected files.

8. Data Retention & How to Delete Your Account

You can permanently delete your account and data anytime using either option:

Option A — Inside the App

Open RoamCeylon or RoamCeylon Admin → Settings → Account → Delete Account.

Option B — By Email

Email privacy@roamceylon.com with the subject “Account Deletion Request”.

Your profile, saved plans, and personal preferences are deleted within 30 days. Completed tax/payment invoices required by Sri Lankan accounting regulations are kept separately for up to 5 years and deleted once that legal period ends.

9. Your Privacy Rights

Under Sri Lanka’s Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA No. 9 of 2022) and international privacy rules, you may request a copy of your data, ask us to fix inaccurate details, withdraw optional permissions, or request full deletion anytime.

10. Children & Youth Safety

The RoamCeylon Traveler App is not intended for children under age 13. The RoamCeylon Admin Partner App is strictly for adults aged 18+ running registered hospitality, tour, or craft businesses.

11. Updates to This Policy

Whenever we update this policy meaningfully, we refresh the date at the top of the page and notify active users inside the apps.
